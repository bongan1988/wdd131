Fibre Optic Communication

Fibre optic communication is a high-speed data transmission technology that uses thin strands of glass or plastic fibres to carry information as pulses of light. It provides faster data transfer, higher bandwidth, and greater reliability than traditional copper cables, making it ideal for internet services, telecommunications, television broadcasting, and long-distance communication.

Fibre Optic vs Copper Cable

Feature Fibre Optic Copper Cable
Speed Very High Moderate
Bandwidth Excellent Limited
Distance Up to 100 km+ About 100 metres
Signal Loss Very Low High
Security High Lower
