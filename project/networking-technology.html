Networking Technology

A computer network is a collection of interconnected devices, such as computers, servers, smartphones, and printers, that communicate and share data, resources, and services through wired or wireless connections. Networks enable users to access the internet, exchange information, share files, and collaborate efficiently across homes, businesses, schools, and organizations.

🌍

WAN

A Wide Area Network connects computers across countries and continents. The Internet is the largest WAN.

🏠

LAN

A Local Area Network connects computers within a limited area such as a home, office, or school.

🏢

MAN

A Metropolitan Area Network connects multiple LANs across a city or metropolitan area.

As technology continues to evolve with innovations such as 5G, cloud computing, the Internet of Things (IoT), and artificial intelligence (AI), networking will remain at the heart of global connectivity. By learning about networking technologies, individuals can develop valuable skills that support careers in telecommunications, information technology, cybersecurity, and network engineering.
